Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

NIKSYNC Space Explorer does not ask you to create an account, and it does not collect your name, email or any personal details inside the app.

What happens when you visit

  • The site is hosted by a third-party hosting provider, which may keep standard server logs such as IP address, browser type and time of visit.
  • The page loads the three.js graphics library from a public content delivery network (cdnjs). That provider can see your IP address when your browser requests the file.
  • The app runs in your browser. The date you choose and the settings you change stay on your device and are not sent to us.

Cookies and analytics

At the time of writing the site does not set its own cookies and does not use analytics. If we add analytics or advertising later, this page will be updated first and will explain what is collected and why.

Contact

For privacy questions, see the contact page.

This is a plain-language policy for a small project and is not legal advice.
