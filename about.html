About NIKSYNC Space Explorer

NIKSYNC Space Explorer is an interactive 3D model of the Solar System that runs in your browser, on a phone or a computer. Rotate any planet through 360°, zoom from deep space down to the surface, take a guided tour, or watch Earth rise over the Moon.

Open the explorer

What you can do

  • Rotate and zoom freely, with pinch, scroll or drag.
  • Pick any date from 1800 to 2050 and see where the planets are on that day.
  • Read live distance, orbital speed and light-travel time for each planet, and the Moon's phase.
  • Take a guided tour or a fast fly-through, and explore Saturn's rings, the asteroid belt, a comet and a black hole visualization.

Where the data and images come from

Planet positions are calculated from NASA/JPL approximate orbital elements (valid for 1800–2050, accurate to roughly arc-minutes; up to about 10 arc-minutes for Jupiter and Saturn). The Moon uses a standard mean-orbit series.

The Sun, planet and Moon surface maps are Solar System Scope textures (CC BY 4.0), based on NASA data. Io, Europa, Ganymede, Callisto, Titan, Enceladus, Triton, the asteroids, the comet and the galaxy are procedural. The black hole is an artistic, analytic approximation, not a general-relativistic simulation. Rendering uses three.js (MIT license).
